Three ways a retention clock starts

Each category of data has a rule. You set the period; SafeData works out the deletion date for every record in it and keeps it up to date.

The clock startsExample categoryExample rule
The day the data is collectedInvoices and payment recordsKept for 6 years from the invoice date, then deleted.
The day consent is withdrawnWebinar or newsletter sign-upsDeleted 7 days after the person asks to be removed.
The day the relationship endsCustomer KYC filesKept for 5 years after the account is closed.

If data is held on the basis of consent and that consent is withdrawn, the record is not kept past your post-withdrawal window, even if its normal period is longer. Records you must keep by law are not shortened.

One webinar, start to finish

A typical consent-based case, the way it runs in SafeData.

  1. Day 0

    Someone registers

    Your registration form sends SafeData a reference for the sign-up through the API. A fingerprint of the email address is enough; SafeData does not need the person's details.

  2. Day 3

    They ask to be removed

    A second form, or your team, records the withdrawal. The record's deletion date is set to day 10 and shows on the Data collection page with its countdown.

  3. Day 10

    The record is disposed

    The nightly run disposes it, notifies your connected system, and writes the action to the audit ledger.

Nothing is deleted without a trail

Preview before anything changes

See exactly which records the next run would flag, dispose or hold for approval. The preview changes nothing and notifies no one.

  • Owners get an in-app notice and an email when a record is within 30 days of its date.
  • Sensitive categories can require one or two approvers.
  • A legal hold or an emergency freeze stops disposal until it is lifted.

An audit ledger you can check

Every flag, approval, withdrawal and disposal is recorded with who did it and when. Each entry is chained to the one before it with SHA-256, so a changed or missing entry shows up when the chain is verified.

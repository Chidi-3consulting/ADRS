How SafeData Works

1. Connect Your Systems

Register your CRM, database, or billing system. Generate API keys and set up auto-categorisation rules.

2. Ingest Data Records

Your systems push data references to SafeData via REST API. Each record is mapped to a retention category automatically.

3. Monitor Retention Clocks

SafeData tracks every record's retention deadline. DPOs and data owners are notified before expiry.

4. Review and Approve

Sensitive categories require dual approval before disposal. Legal holds can freeze the clock at any time.

5. Automated Disposal

SafeData sends signed deletion instructions to your systems. Every action is recorded in a tamper-proof audit ledger.

INTEGRATION FLOW

Connects to your existing infrastructure

SafeData does not store your actual data. It tracks references to your data records and tells your systems when to delete, anonymise, or archive them.

Inbound: Data Intake

Your system sends a record reference to SafeData when data is collected. SafeData assigns a retention category and starts the clock.

Lifecycle: Monitoring

SafeData monitors retention deadlines daily. Lifecycle events like account closures or consent withdrawals adjust the timeline automatically.

Outbound: Disposal

When a record's retention period expires, SafeData sends a cryptographically signed webhook to your system instructing it to delete or anonymise the data.

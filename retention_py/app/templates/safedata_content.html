The SafeData lifecycle

A clear process for responsible data lifecycle management

01

Understand

Build a clear inventory of information assets, ownership, and the business context in which records are held.

02

Set the schedule

Document retention periods and review dates against your organisation’s approved policies and obligations.

03

Review and decide

Give authorised people a checkpoint to review records before a retention or disposal action proceeds.

04

Record the evidence

Keep an auditable record of decisions and actions so your team can explain what happened and why.

Governance support—not legal advice.

SafeData helps teams operationalise their approved policies. Your organisation remains responsible for confirming applicable laws, setting retention schedules, and authorising disposal.

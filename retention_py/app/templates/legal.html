Legal Terms & Compliance Framework

3Consulting SafeData — Data Retention & Governance Platform Terms of Service & Privacy Statement.

1. Compliance Alignment

SafeData is built to enforce automated data lifecycle policies in accordance with global data protection standards, including EU/UK GDPR, Nigeria NDPR/NDPA, California CCPA/CPRA, South Africa POPIA, Brazil LGPD, and regional privacy frameworks.

2. Append-Only Audit Integrity

All operational events, records state transitions, and compliance verification actions are recorded in an append-only audit trail protected by cryptographic SHA-256 HMAC row hashes.

3. Data Subject Rights (DSAR)

The solution supports automated lifecycle adjustments for consent withdrawal (Article 7 GDPR / NDPR), erasure requests (Right to be Forgotten), and statutory legal holds for ongoing litigation.

Slide 1 • Market Context

The Commercial Problem: Data Hoarding Liability

Corporations face massive fines not for losing data, but for holding it past legal retention dates.

The Enterprise Pain Point

  • The "Data Hoarder's Dilemma": Companies retain customer records indefinitely because deleting data manually across databases carries high business anxiety.
  • Skyrocketing Regulatory Fines: NDPA (Nigeria, up to 2% annual turnover or £10m), GDPR (€20m or 4% turnover), POPIA, CCPA actively penalize over-retention.
  • Legal Hold & Audit Failures: In audits or litigation, teams scramble manually through spreadsheets, creating severe defensibility gaps.
  • Controller vs. Processor Blindspot: Banks and telcos cannot verify if their 3rd-party vendors actually deleted customer data.

The SafeData Solution

Automated 9-Step Lifecycle

Calculates statutory expiry dates per jurisdiction, notifies record owners, and executes verified disposals with dual-approval safeguards.

Provable Cryptographic Proof

SHA-256 hash-chained audit ledger proves to regulators that data was retained lawfully and disposed of on schedule.

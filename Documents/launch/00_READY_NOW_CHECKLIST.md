# SafeData — What to Get Ready Now

## Brand and positioning

- Finalise the product name and endorsement line: SafeData by 3Consulting
- Use the approved tagline: “Keep what you must. Release what you should.”
- Use the secondary line: “We govern your data without hoarding it.”
- Confirm the Lifecycle Ring as the mark, and keep the colour direction consistent with the parent 3Consulting brand family.
- Align all sales and pitch materials to the core promise: lawful retention, provable disposal, and compliance without data hoarding.

## Governance and legal readiness

- Decide who owns the product IP, the commercial operation, and any future customer data responsibilities.
- Confirm the company structure for payments: launch on Paystack for Nigerian and African transactions, while evaluating UK limited company or Merchant of Record options for Stripe and international billing.
- Confirm whether a standalone domain should be registered now and whether the launch domain remains safedata.3consult-ng.com or moves later.
- Prepare a support policy: email support with one-business-day response, with customer-approved access only for genuine support scenarios.
- Set the customer promise clearly: no data hostage model, no hidden lockout after grace period, export and approval remain available in read-only mode.

## Operational readiness

- Prepare the exact compliance scope for launch: retention engine, tamper-evident audit chain, tenant separation, default secure config, backup and restore process.
- Confirm the minimum environment for launch: Cloudflare, managed Postgres, object storage, encrypted backups, error monitoring, and a hosted support process.
- Finalise the launch environment model: managed cloud hosting in Europe with a staged deployment path to a public domain.
- Check the platform bootstrap process: a single Platform Owner, verified admin bootstrap, no shared passwords, mandatory 2FA, and restricted platform admin access.
- Define the support and incident escalation process for deployment, maintenance, and customer access requests.

## Product readiness now

- Complete the security and tenancy foundation before taking any real customer data.
- Finalise the data model for the product hierarchy: Group → Entity → Unit.
- Lock the role and permission model before exposing any organisation to live users.
- Complete the billing controls before the public trial is launched.
- Complete the consent and governance screens before promising any full-suite workflow.
- Decide the minimum launch scope and what is explicitly not in scope until Phase 2–6.

## Smart next-step checklist

### Get ready in the next 2 weeks

1. Finalise brand lockup and taglines in every sales and product asset.
2. Approve the launch domain and hosting model.
3. Finalise payment and legal ownership route.
4. Validate the security and tenancy gap list and assign owners for each fix.
5. Prepare a shortlist of pilot customers or early adopter organisations.

### Get ready in the next 30 days

1. Complete the Phase 1B tenancy and permissions work.
2. Complete the compliance core and audit integrity work.
3. Finalise the billing plan and trial lifecycle rules.
4. Prepare launch pricing, sales pages, and trust centre content.
5. Build a proof pack for regulators and auditors.

### Get ready before commercial launch

1. Perform the Phase 6 re-audit and security verification.
2. Run the pen test and restore drill.
3. Publish the final trust, help, and legal centre pages.
4. Turn on billing, reminders, and support flows in test mode.
5. Launch with only the secure, approved scope.

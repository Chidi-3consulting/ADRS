## Slide 3 — Next steps roadmap

### Phase 1B — tenancy and permissions

- Group → Entity → Unit model
- Role-based access control by scope
- Platform-owner bootstrap and admin controls
- Secure support access and audit record of actions

### Phase 1C — compliance core

- Jurisdiction rules as cited data
- Accurate legal clock arithmetic
- Per-organisation tamper-evident audit chain
- Safer disposal and evidence export controls

### Phase 2 — design system, onboarding, help

- Lifecycle Ring brand system
- Shell, navigation, message patterns, role homes
- Guided onboarding and next-best-step journeys
- Help centre and support model

### Phase 3 — billing and commercial controls

- Pricing catalogue and regional bands
- Trial, read-only, cancellation, and invoice flow
- Paystack and Stripe adapter model
- Billing state and compliance governance

### Phase 4 — Consent and requests

- Consent capture and withdrawal flow
- Hosted preference centre

### Phase 5 — governance modules

- DPIA, gap assessments, breach register, RoPA, policy generator

### Phase 6 — re-audit and go-live

- Security review, documentation, and readiness signoff
- Production deployment and go-live checklist

## Slide 4 — Profit maximization model

### Revenue streams beyond subscriptions

- SaaS subscriptions: Retention, Consent, and Suite
- Expert services: DPIA, gap assessments, DPO-as-a-service retainers
- MSP and reseller partnerships: white-label and referral channel
- Public gap check: lead generation and conversion funnel
- Training and certification: courses, workshops, and compliance enablement
- Add-ons: more entities, integrations, API usage, advanced analytics
- Template packs and country rule packs
- Marketplace listings and integration partnerships

### Why this scales

- The platform is not just a tool; it is a compliance operating system
- Regulation drives repeat demand in every industry
- 3Consulting can layer expertise services on top of software
- The same product can be sold to established enterprises and growing organisations

### Commercial direction

- Launch the core product first
- Package services as add-ons around the product
- Convert organisations through trust and compliance proof, not fear-based marketing
- Expand into channel and partner growth after product-market fit
